#PlatoonX — Goal, Vision & Mission

#Goal

To advance India’s technological capabilities through world-class artificial intelligence research, the creation of original AI technologies, and the translation of scientific discoveries into high-impact real-world applications.

#Vision

To establish India as a global leader in artificial intelligence research, innovation, and technological development, contributing original knowledge and AI technologies that address significant challenges across science, industry, and society.

#Mission

To conduct fundamental and applied research across artificial intelligence and related fields; develop novel algorithms, models, intelligent systems, and AI-driven technologies; and translate research outcomes into practical solutions for India's scientific, industrial, economic, and societal needs. PlatoonX aims to cultivate a research-driven culture that advances the frontiers of AI while strengthening India's capacity to create, own, and deploy globally competitive AI technologies.
